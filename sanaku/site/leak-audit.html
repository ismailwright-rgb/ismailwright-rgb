How The Catch Works

Four workflows that stop the bleed

01

Missed-Call Text-Back

A call goes unanswered → within seconds the caller gets a text from your number: “Sorry we missed you — what do you need help with?” The reply is captured, qualified, and you're alerted.

A plumber missing 6 calls a week who recovers just 2 jobs has already paid for the entire service.
$250/mo · $500 setup
02

After-Hours Intake

When the office is closed, a smart form or chat asks the three questions that qualify the lead, then books the appointment or escalates urgent work to your on-call phone.

Typically 30–45% of captured leads arrive when the office is closed.
$200/mo · $400 setup
03

Lead Nurture Sequences

Five-touch follow-up for everyone who inquired and went quiet. Most businesses follow up once and stop. The sale usually happens on touch three or four.

Re-engages the 60%+ of inquiries that never got a second touch.
$225/mo · $500 setup

Appointment Reminders & No-Show Recovery

Confirmations at booking, reminders at 24h and 1h, plus an automatic “life happens — want to rebook?” message the morning after a no-show.

Cutting no-shows from 20% to 10% at a dental practice recovers thousands per month.
$175/mo · $350 setup

The Fine Print, Up Front

What we won't do

No cold robocalls. Ever.

Automated voices only ever call people who just contacted you, about the thing they contacted you about. Cold AI calling carries $500–$1,500 per call in penalties, and it is your number on the caller ID, not ours.

Everyone knows what they're talking to.

The agent says it is automated and that the call is recorded, before it asks anything. California is a two-party consent state and we treat that as the floor, not the ceiling.

Stop means stop.

Every text honours an opt-out immediately and permanently. Anyone who replies to a sequence drops out of it and comes straight to you — a human reply never gets answered by a robot.

It's your brand on everything.

Your number, your business name, your voice, your colours in the reporting portal your team logs into. Nothing your customers see says Sanaku.

Healthcare gets a BAA first.

If patients are involved, every vendor in the chain is covered by a signed business associate agreement before a single call is answered. Messages carry appointment details and nothing clinical.

You can switch it off in one click.

There is a pause button in your portal. No notice period, no phone call to cancel, no data held hostage. If it stops earning its keep, turn it off.
