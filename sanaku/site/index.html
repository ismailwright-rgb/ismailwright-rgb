Questions owners actually ask

Fair questions, straight answers

Will my customers know it's automated?

The texts and emails come from your number and your name, written in plain language — most customers just think you're impressively fast. And the moment a conversation needs a human, it's routed to one: you or your staff, immediately.

How long does setup take?

The flagship missed-call text-back is typically live within a week of our call. We install, test with your own phone, and show you the first captured leads before you pay a second invoice.

Do I have to rip out my current phone system or website?

No. The workflows sit alongside what you already have — your existing number, your existing site. We add the catching layer; we don't rebuild your stack.

Is the texting legal and compliant?

Yes — that's load-bearing in how we build. Messages go to people who called or contacted you first, from properly registered business numbers, with opt-outs honored instantly and permanently. For law and healthcare clients, our fee structures are built to respect fee-splitting and anti-kickback rules from day one.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

Every captured lead is logged — so within a month you'll see exactly what the system caught, including what came in after hours. If the meter doesn't justify the fee, cancel. The arithmetic either works or it doesn't; we're comfortable letting it decide.
